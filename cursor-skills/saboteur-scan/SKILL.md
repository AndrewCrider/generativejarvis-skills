# Saboteur Scan

Identify the user's top 1–2 saboteurs from the Positive Intelligence (PQ) framework and map them to their specific AI workflow costs and fixes.

---

## Required inputs

Present these 10 behavioral patterns as a numbered list. Ask the user to pick the 2–3 that feel most true — especially under stress or in high-stakes moments:

1. **Judge** — Quick to criticize yourself when things don't go perfectly. Inner voice is harsh.
2. **Avoider** — Focus on the positive; steer away from conflict, hard conversations, or uncomfortable tasks.
3. **Controller** — Feel anxious when things are out of your control. Prefer to do it yourself rather than delegate.
4. **Hyper-Achiever** — Self-worth tied to output. A slow day feels like a failure, even when rest was necessary.
5. **Hyper-Rational** — Default to logic. Emotional or intuitive decisions feel sloppy.
6. **Hyper-Vigilant** — Always scanning for what could go wrong. Best-case scenarios feel naive until proven real.
7. **Pleaser** — Hard time saying no. Help others before yourself; often overcommitted before noticing.
8. **Restless** — Genuinely excited by new ideas; lose interest once something is figured out.
9. **Stickler** — High standards. Vague plans and half-finished work are uncomfortable. Want it right, not fast.
10. **Victim** — Strong emotional reactions when things don't go your way. Sometimes feels like the world happens *to* you.

---

For each of their top 2 saboteurs (or 1 if they only picked 1), output this block:
