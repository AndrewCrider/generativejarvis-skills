# Weekly OS

A 5-minute weekly planning ritual. 5 questions → structured week plan → saboteur call-out.

---

## Required inputs (ask one at a time — do not batch)

**Q1:** What are your top 2–3 active projects or areas of responsibility right now?

**Q2:** What was your biggest win from last week? One sentence. Even if the week was rough.

**Q3:** What blocked you most last week — the one thing that cost you the most time, energy, or focus?

**Q4:** Energy level going into this week? (1 = running on fumes / 5 = locked in)

**Q5:** If this week were successful, what 3 things would be done? Not nice-to-haves — if you only did these 3, you'd call it a good week.

---

Output this exact structure:

---

**Win:** [Q2 answer — affirm it in one short specific sentence]
**Block:** [Q3 answer — reframe as a question: "What would it take to unblock [thing] this week?"]

**Must-do 1:** [Q5 item 1]
**Must-do 2:** [Q5 item 2]
**Must-do 3:** [Q5 item 3]

### Stretch goals (only if must-dos are complete)
[2 plausible stretch goals from their Q1 projects — clearly secondary]

### One thing to protect
> "Protect **[the must-do most at risk of displacement by urgent-but-unimportant work]**. Schedule it first."

[One-paragraph call-out based on Q3 block — pick from mapping below]

## Saboteur → block mapping (internal)

| If the block was... | Saboteur | Call-out |
|--------------------|----------|----------|
| New ideas / shiny tools / distraction | Restless | "Watch for Restless this week. Park new ideas in an Idea Bank — don't let them displace your 3 must-dos." |
| Couldn't finish — not good enough | Stickler / Judge | "Watch for Stickler. Set a 'good enough' bar before you start [must-do]. Ship when you hit it." |
| Avoided hard conversation / difficult task | Avoider | "Watch for Avoider. Name the conversation or decision you're avoiding. Put it on your calendar." |
| Spent time on others' priorities | Pleaser | "Watch for Pleaser. Your must-dos belong to you. Say no to one non-list request this week." |
| Worried about what could go wrong | Hyper-Vigilant | "Watch for Hyper-Vigilant. You've already done the planning. Trust it. Worst-case thinking is overhead." |
| Kept taking work back from others/AI | Controller | "Watch for Controller. Pick one must-do to delegate or AI-assist without reviewing the result twice." |
| Pushed through when you should've rested | Hyper-Achiever | "Watch for Hyper-Achiever. At energy [Q4 score], a productive week looks different than a peak week. Adjust." |
| Couldn't start / analysis / overthinking | Hyper-Rational | "Watch for Hyper-Rational. You have enough info to start [must-do 1]. Start anyway." |
| Strong emotional reaction to last week | Victim | "Watch for Victim. Last week is over. What would you do differently if it hadn't happened?" |
| Self-criticism about last week | Judge | "Watch for Judge. Your win ([Q2]) was real. Don't let the inner critic rewrite last week as a failure." |

If block doesn't clearly map, pick closest and note it's an approximation.
